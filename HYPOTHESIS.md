# Locked Pre-Test Evaluation Protocol & Hypothesis (`disRated`)

> **Protocol Lock & Provenance Note:** This protocol was locked **prior to unsealing `splits.v2.json["test"]`** for any Tier-2 VLM inference. Because Section 4 was amended after Tier-2 validation (`val`) runs were completed (adding validation Platt calibration and aligning `k*` selection with Section 5's CI criteria), we explicitly avoid calling this externally "pre-registered" (no third-party timestamp such as OSF). Instead, Phase 4 (`disrated eval-test`) reports held-out `test` outcomes under **both** the amended rule (`k* = 0.30`, Platt-calibrated) and the original rule (`k* = 0.05`, uncalibrated) for full transparency. No new routing or fusion mechanisms (e.g., log-odds fusion or learned routers) are added prior to Phase 4.

## 1. Scope, Dataset Provenance & External Validity Bounds
- **Benchmark:** VHD11K (10,000 images: 5,000 web `concept` across 45 tags + 5,000 synthetic `sdxl`, balanced 50.0% harmful / 50.0% safe; binary `decision` label generated by a 3-VLM debate judge, reported at ~82.5% human agreement on SMID in the VHD11K paper).
- **Framing & Selection-Into-Ambiguity:** This study evaluates selective classification, model complementarity (oracle-gap decomposition), and compute-triage measuring agreement with VHD11K's debate judge. No claims are made regarding objective ground-truth safety beyond judge agreement. Because VHD11K pairs harmful images 1:1 against visually matched hard negatives (`all_10000_evenHarmfulUnharmful`) and includes broad normative concepts alongside concrete visual harm, this benchmark models **post-pre-filter semantic triage** (~2–5% suspect stream) rather than raw ~1%-prevalence platform traffic. Furthermore, uncertainty routing (`|logit(p_probe)| < c`) concentrates both context-dependent visual edge cases and normatively contested judge labels (`H(Y|X) >> 0`), so Tier-2 in-band accuracy must always be interpreted **paired against Tier 0 on the exact same escalated subset** and alongside **out-of-band Tier-2 accuracy** and **union oracle headroom**.
- **Active Splits & Connected Components:** `splits.v2.json` (`sha256: 07b1776d1d8ec1949e799859fe321e993361d6998040534601f81e4b0e3b09f2`), 8,000 `train` / 1,000 `val` / 1,000 `test`, grouped over 8,449 isolated connected components (`embeddings/components.json`, `sha256: d67fd10c8d50287fb3016176064a4bf476e02104e614eb5cb51864851d1870f3`, tracked in git to eliminate cross-GPU MPS/CUDA floating-point drift at the `0.95` cosine boundary).
- **Sealed-Test Boundary:** Tier-0 baselines (`clip-b16-v2`, `clip-l14`, RQ2 fine-tuned models, CLIP zero-shot) and zero-learning dummy baselines (`constant-0`, `constant-1`, `random`) were logged on `splits.v2.json["test"]` in Phases 1–2 prior to Tier-2 integration. `splits.v2.json["test"]` remains **strictly sealed** for all Tier-2 VLM inference, prompt design, probability calibration, and operating-point selection (`c*`, `z_vlm_cut*`) until Phase 4 is explicitly unsealed.

## 2. Experimental Arms
All arms are evaluated on the exact same validation ($N = 1,000$) and held-out test ($N = 1,000$) partitions:
1. **`probe-only` (Tier 0 Baseline):** $L_2$-regularised logistic regression ($C = 1.0$, tuned on `val`) fit on frozen `openai/clip-vit-base-patch16` (`rev: 57c21647`) `train` features. Hard decisions taken at $p \ge 0.50$ ($\text{logit}(p) \ge 0$).
2. **`vlm-only` (Tier 2 Baseline):** Zero-shot Vision-Language Model evaluated across 100% of split images with blind pixel ingest (`load_clean()`). Continuous scores are extracted as raw binary decision logits $z_{\text{vlm}}$ (unclipped $\log P(\text{Yes}) - \log P(\text{No})$ when recorded in `extra["logit_diff"]`, else $\text{logit}(p_{\text{vlm}})$) with `verdict = None`. On standalone `vlm-only` and in-band VLM accuracy, refusals are scored as incorrect predictions (never credited via Tier-0 fallback).
3. **`cascade` (Proposed):** One-knob uncertainty routing around the probe decision boundary. For a scalar cutoff $c > 0$ on $\vert\text{logit}(p_{\text{probe}})\vert$:
   - If $\vert\text{logit}(p_{\text{probe}})\vert \ge c$: emit `probe-only` hard decision.
   - If $\vert\text{logit}(p_{\text{probe}})\vert < c$: escalate image to `vlm-only` and emit the VLM's hard decision (falling back to `probe-only` on escalated refusals, while logging refusal rate as a secondary metric).
4. **`random-control` (Gating Control):** Escalates the exact same number of images $N_{\text{esc}}$ as `cascade` on the evaluated split, chosen uniformly at random without replacement. To eliminate single-seed routing variance, `random-control` is evaluated as the **Monte-Carlo expectation over $R = 200$ deterministic uniform routing masks** (`seed = 0`), computed jointly inside every bootstrap replicate.

## 3. Primary Metric, Oracle-Gap Decomposition & Statistical Testing
- **Primary Metric:** Binary `macro_f1` against `decision`, defined as $\frac{1}{2}(F_{1,\text{harmful}} + F_{1,\text{safe}})$.
- **Oracle-Gap & Complementarity Diagnostics:** Alongside the pass/falsification tests in §5, we report overall and in-band **union oracle accuracy** (`(probe right) OR (VLM right)`) and the **fraction of the `probe-only -> union_oracle` headroom captured** by 1-knob confidence routing, quantifying how much remaining error stems from imperfect routing versus VHD11K label noise (estimated via blind human audit on `val`).
- **Secondary Metrics:** Overall accuracy, in-band accuracy (VLM vs. probe), out-of-band VLM accuracy, resolved probe accuracy, realised escalation rate, VLM refusal rate, per-source (`concept` vs. `sdxl`) `macro_f1`, and locked concept-taxonomy slices.
- **Locked Concept Taxonomy Partition (defined prior to Phase 4 from tag names alone):**
  - **`concrete_harm` (22 tags):** `animal_abuse`, `animal_genitalia_and_human`, `animal_genitalia_only`, `animated_animal_genitalia`, `animated_blood`, `animated_corpse`, `animated_gun`, `bloody`, `fetish_extremes`, `gun`, `hanging`, `human_corpse`, `knife_in_hand`, `nazi`, `noose`, `nudity`, `sex_toy`, `sexual_activity`, `shocking`, `terrorist`, `theft`, `violence`.
  - **`contextual_normative` (23 tags):** `alcohol`, `animated_alcohol`, `bulge`, `cheating`, `cleavage`, `contamination`, `cruelty_in_the_animal_kingdom`, `culinary_knife_in_hand`, `emaciated_body`, `gambling`, `harrassment`, `inequality`, `injectables`, `lack_of_self_control`, `marijuana`, `obesity`, `obscene_gesture`, `pills`, `religious_icon`, `rudeness`, `sexual_intent`, `smoking`, `toy`.
- **Uncertainty Estimation:** 95% confidence intervals computed via **paired component-cluster non-parametric bootstrap** ($B = 10,000$ resamples of connected-component IDs from `embeddings/components.json` with replacement, `seed = 0`), preserving within-component correlation (294/1,000 `val` and 293/1,000 `test` images belong to multi-image components). Standard i.i.d. image-level bootstrap intervals are also logged as a sensitivity check.

## 4. Validation Calibration, Threshold Selection Rule & Locked Phase 4 Models
All calibration and threshold selection rules are executed exclusively on `val` ($N = 1,000$) prior to touching `test`:

1. **Validation Platt Calibration (`z_vlm_cut*`):** Instruction-tuned VLMs can exhibit conservative decision bias and logit overconfidence under zero-shot binary prompting. Fit a 2-parameter logistic calibration (Platt scaling: $\sigma(a z_{\text{vlm}} + b)$) on `val` VLM logits $z_{\text{vlm}}$ (unclipped `extra["logit_diff"]` when available) to obtain calibrated decision threshold $z_{\text{platt}} = -b / a$ ($\tau_{\text{platt}} = \sigma(z_{\text{platt}})$). Under `--tau-mode auto`, lock $z_{\text{vlm}}^* = z_{\text{platt}}$ if Platt calibration strictly improves standalone `val` VLM `macro_f1` over uncalibrated $z \ge 0$ ($\tau = 0.50$), else retain $z_{\text{vlm}}^* = 0.0$. For full transparency, Phase 4 (`disrated eval-test`) reports **both** the locked calibrated operating point and the uncalibrated ($z \ge 0$) reference on `test`.
2. **Candidate Cutoffs:** Define candidate cutoffs $c_k = \text{Quantile}(\vert\text{logit}(p_{\text{val}})\vert, k)$ for $k \in \{0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.25, 0.30\}$.
3. **Pre-Test Validation Gate (In-Band Superiority):** On `val`, at candidate cutoff $c_k$, the VLM's accuracy on the escalated band ($\vert\text{logit}(p_{\text{val}})\vert < c_k$) must strictly exceed the probe's accuracy on the exact same subset. If no candidate beats the probe in-band on `val`, the hypothesis is marked falsified at the gate.
4. **CI-Aligned Operating Point Selection (Amended Rule):** Among candidate values $k \in \{0.05, \dots, 0.30\}$ passing the Validation Gate:
   - Identify candidates whose `val` paired component-cluster 95% CIs satisfy all three validation analogues of §5.2–§5.4: `cascade_minus_probe` CI lower bound $> 0$, `cascade_minus_vlm` CI lower bound $\ge -0.030$, and `cascade_minus_random` CI lower bound $> 0$.
   - Among qualifying candidates, select the $k^*$ that **maximises `val` cascade `macro_f1`** (breaking ties in favour of smaller $k$).
   - **Fallback:** If no candidate satisfies all three `val` CI bounds simultaneously, select the gate-passing $k \in \{0.05, \dots, 0.30\}$ that maximises `val` cascade `macro_f1`.
   - **Original-Rule Transparency:** Because this rule was amended after `val` VLM outputs existed (replacing the original rule *"smallest $k$ within $0.030$ of VLM-only and exceeding probe-only"*, which locked uncalibrated $k^* = 0.05$, $c^* = 0.122956$), Phase 4 also logs `test` results at the original $(k^* = 0.05, z_{\text{vlm}} = 0.0)$ operating point alongside the amended lock.
5. **Locked Phase 4 Tier-2 Models (frozen prior to unsealing `test`):**
   - **Primary Tier-2 VLM:** **`Qwen/Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct-FP8`** (`rev: 9cdc6310a8cb770ce18efaf4e9935334512aee45`, CUDA FP8 on `vorlab` RTX 5060 Ti). Locked gate: `results/phase3_val_gate_qwen3_8b.json` — amended lock `k* = 0.30`, `c* = 0.659363`, `z_vlm_cut* = -6.5590` (`tau_vlm* = 0.00142`, `val` cascade Macro-F1 `0.8039` vs. `vlm-only` `0.8130`, `probe-only` `0.7639`, `union_oracle_all = 0.878`).
   - **Secondary Small-VLM Comparison:** **`LiquidAI/d1-2B-MLX-fp16`** (`rev: f4e6e118bf850880e6f45a5b22085057778ac243`, Apple Silicon MLX). Locked gate: `results/phase3_val_gate.json` — amended lock `k* = 0.30`, `c* = 0.659363`, `z_vlm_cut* = -0.5070` (`tau_vlm* = 0.37590`, `val` cascade Macro-F1 `0.7970` vs. `vlm-only` `0.7820`, `probe-only` `0.7639`, `union_oracle_all = 0.889`).
6. **Immutable Transfer to Test (`disrated eval-test`):** Lock $(c^*, z_{\text{vlm}}^*)$ alongside SHA-256 digests of `splits.v2.json`, `components.json`, model/probe metadata, and validation prediction files in the Phase 3 gate JSON, and apply $(c^*, z_{\text{vlm}}^*)$ **unchanged** to `test`. The test escalation rate $k_{\text{test}}$ is an empirical outcome, never recomputed as a quantile on `test`.

## 5. Pass / Falsification Criteria (Held-Out Test Split)
Using primary component-cluster paired bootstrap CIs ($B = 10,000$, `seed = 0`, Monte-Carlo `random-control` with $R = 200$) at the locked operating point $(c^*, z_{\text{vlm}}^*)$ for the primary Tier-2 VLM (`Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct-FP8`), the cascade hypothesis passes on `test` if and only if the Phase 3 validation gate passed **and all five** conditions hold:
1. **Budget Cap:** Realised test escalation rate $k_{\text{test}} \le 0.300$.
2. **Superiority over Probe-Only:** Paired difference $\Delta_{\text{probe}} = \text{Macro-}F_1(\text{cascade}) - \text{Macro-}F_1(\text{probe-only}) > 0$, with 95% cluster-bootstrap CI lower bound $> 0$.
3. **Non-Inferiority to VLM-Only:** Paired difference $\Delta_{\text{vlm}} = \text{Macro-}F_1(\text{cascade}) - \text{Macro-}F_1(\text{vlm-only})$ has 95% cluster-bootstrap CI lower bound $\ge -0.030$.
4. **Superiority over Random Routing:** Paired difference $\Delta_{\text{rand}} = \text{Macro-}F_1(\text{cascade}) - \text{Macro-}F_1(\text{random-control}) > 0$, with 95% cluster-bootstrap CI lower bound $> 0$.
5. **In-Band Gain:** On the escalated test subset ($\vert\text{logit}(p_{\text{test}})\vert < c^*$), VLM accuracy strictly exceeds probe accuracy.

If any criterion fails, the cascade hypothesis is falsified and reported directly as a negative or bounded empirical finding alongside the exact paired effect sizes, oracle-gap fraction captured, i.i.d. sensitivity intervals, original-rule (`k* = 0.05`) reference, uncalibrated reference, and secondary `d1-2B` comparison.
